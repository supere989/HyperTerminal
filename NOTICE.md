The bundled HyperTerminal keyboard table is adapted from the KDE Konsole default
keyboard table. Original upstream comments are retained. HyperTerminal adds the
Ctrl+Alt+Backtick mapping and changes the table name.
Upstream: https://invent.kde.org/utilities/konsole/-/blob/master/data/keyboard-layouts/default.keytab

Yakuake, Konsole, tmux, and agent CLIs are separate dependencies; their names,
D-Bus interfaces, and attribution remain intact. They are not bundled here.
